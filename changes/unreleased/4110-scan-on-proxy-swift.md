- **Scan-on-proxy now gates source archives from Swift Remote and Virtual repositories** (#4110, part of #4097). With `scan_on_proxy` on, a proxied `{scope}/{name}/{version}.zip` goes through the shared inline scan-and-block gate instead of streaming unscanned: a vulnerable archive is refused with 403 `scan_blocked`, a clean one is served with `X-AK-Scan: clean`, the verdict is reused for the same digest, and `fail_open`, `fail_closed` and `record_only` behave as they do for the other enforced formats. The archive must be a readable zip with a `Package.swift` at its root or under its one top-level directory; one that is not is withheld under `fail_closed` (423) without being scanned. The bundled scanner has no Swift advisory data, so a source archive is graded on its contents and is only found vulnerable for a vendored component of an ecosystem the scanner does grade. On Remote and Virtual Swift repositories, a source archive scope, name or version containing `?`, `#`, `;`, `\`, a stray `%`, a space or a control character after decoding is now refused with `400`. Release metadata and `Package.swift` are unchanged, and `GET /api/v1/formats` now reports Swift as `scan_on_proxy: "enforced"`.
