section: Changed
issues: [#1683, #4323]
---
- **OCI manifest reads by digest, the referrers API and storage GC now key on the `oci_manifests` existence table** (#1683). On a hosted repository, `GET`, `HEAD` and `DELETE /v2/<name>/manifests/<digest>` resolve through the repository's record of the manifest instead of inferring it from tags and edge rows, and the #1681 content-addressable fallback is gone. The untagged child of a tagged index keeps pulling by digest after its own tag is deleted. Deleting a subject's last tag, or the subject itself, detaches its referrers, so `GET /v2/<name>/referrers/<digest>` of a deleted subject lists nothing. Storage GC keeps a manifest object, and the blob refs of an index's child, while the manifest is recorded, so the child of an untagged index that still pulls by digest is no longer reclaimed. The reconcile now keeps the rows of an index of indexes, of a migrated index's children and of a manifest whose tag moved while its blob refs last, and adds rows for such manifests that have none. See `docs/operations/oci-manifests-reconcile.md` for what a mixed 1.11/1.12 fleet answers.
- **Hosted OCI blob pulls verify the stored bytes against their digest** (Part of #4323). A layer or config blob whose stored object no longer hashes to its digest is cut short instead of served to completion, as format-native downloads already are.
