- **Quarantine decisions, promotion approvals and promotions, permission rules, SSO providers, signing-key rotation and revocation, webhooks and sync policies now write audit rows** (#4710). None of these handlers recorded anything in `audit_log`, so an auditor could not find who released a held package, who approved or ran a promotion, who granted a permission, who changed an identity provider or who rotated a signing key. Each now writes one row naming the acting principal and the target, with the before/after that matters in `details`: `QUARANTINE_RELEASED` and `QUARANTINE_REJECTED`; `PROMOTION_APPROVAL_GRANTED` and `PROMOTION_APPROVAL_DENIED`; `ARTIFACT_PROMOTED`, `PROMOTION_REFUSED` (refused by the quality gate, the promotion policy or a promotion rule, with a summary of the gate results) and `PROMOTION_REJECTED`; `PERMISSION_RULE_CREATED`, `_UPDATED` and `_DELETED`; `SSO_PROVIDER_CREATED`, `_UPDATED`, `_DELETED`, `_ENABLED` and `_DISABLED`; `SIGNING_KEY_ROTATED` and `SIGNING_KEY_REVOKED`; `WEBHOOK_CREATED`, `_UPDATED`, `_DELETED` and `WEBHOOK_SECRET_ROTATED`; `SYNC_POLICY_CREATED`, `_UPDATED` and `_DELETED`. No row carries a secret: client secrets, bind passwords, key material and webhook secrets are never recorded, only that a credential was replaced or a secret rotated, and a webhook URL is recorded without its userinfo or query string. The actions and their `details` are listed in `docs/audit-events.md`.
