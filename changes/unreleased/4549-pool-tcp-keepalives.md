section: Fixed

- **Postgres sessions of a force-killed backend pod are reaped in about 30 s instead of after the OS's ~2 h TCP default, and the backend now retries its initial pool connect instead of crash-looping on the first timeout** (#4549). A pod deleted without a chance to close its sockets leaves ghost sessions behind; across replicas those ghosts plus the surviving pools filled `max_connections` and refused every connection the replacement pod opened (`FATAL: sorry, too many clients already`), so the replacement died on `Sqlx(PoolTimedOut)` and re-entered CrashLoopBackOff for hours. The pool now sets `tcp_keepalives_idle=15`, `tcp_keepalives_interval=5` and `tcp_keepalives_count=3` with a query right after connect on **every** connection it opens — pooled and detached single-flight lock sessions alike — so the server declares a dead peer and ends its session after about `idle + interval * count` = 30 s (#4013 armed only dedicated lock sessions). They are not sent as startup options, which PgBouncer refuses by default; a failure to set them is logged once and never fails the connection, and the new `DATABASE_TCP_KEEPALIVES=false` turns the hook off (for example behind a pooler in transaction mode, where the setting may not stick). Startup retries the initial connect up to five times with a jittered doubling backoff (1–8 s, each attempt bounded by `DATABASE_ACQUIRE_TIMEOUT_SECS`, no new attempt after about 60 s, so a database that stays down fails the process in about 40 s with the defaults) instead of exiting on the first `PoolTimedOut`, and the final error names the connection budget: `DATABASE_MAX_CONNECTIONS × replicas`, plus out-of-pool single-flight connections (`PROXY_SINGLEFLIGHT_MAX_LOCK_WAITERS` per replica), plus one rolling-update surge replica must stay below Postgres `max_connections` — now documented in `.env.example`, whose shipped defaults (3 × 50 = 150) exceed a stock server's 100. Every failure is counted in the new `ak_db_pool_connection_failures_total{stage="startup"|"request"}` so saturation is visible in Prometheus before queries fail.
